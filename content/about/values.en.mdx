### Ubuntu

We believe in our shared humanity and collective responsibility. We foster solidarity among citizen observers and democratic actors, recognizing that the strength of civic space in one African country contributes to the strength of democracy across the continent.

### Transparency

We are open and clear about our methodologies, processes, data, and findings. We make our work accessible to scrutiny, enabling stakeholders to understand, verify, and build upon our evidence.

### Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, impartiality, professionalism, and evidence-based practice. We hold ourselves to the same standards of integrity that we expect from the electoral processes we observe.

### Accountability

We take responsibility for the accuracy of our data and the impact of our recommendations, to the observers we represent and the public we serve.

### Non-partisanship

We take responsibility for our actions, findings, and commitments. We strive to ensure that our data and assessments are accurate, our work is credible, and our recommendations serve the interests of citizens and the public good.

### Inclusion

We maintain independence from political parties, candidates, governments, and other partisan interests. Our assessments are guided by evidence, established standards, and democratic principles, ensuring that our work remains credible and trusted by diverse stakeholders.
