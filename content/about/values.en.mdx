### Ubuntu

We act in the spirit of shared humanity and solidarity — recognizing that the strength of civic space in one African nation is bound to the strength of civic space across the continent.

### Transparency

We are open about our methodology, our data, and our findings, so that our work can be scrutinized, trusted, and built upon.

### Integrity

We hold ourselves to the same standards of honesty and rigor that we expect from the electoral processes we observe.

### Accountability

We take responsibility for the accuracy of our data and the impact of our recommendations, to the observers we represent and the public we serve.

### Non-partisanship

We remain independent of political parties, governments, and candidates, so that our assessments are trusted by all sides.

### Inclusion

We amplify the voices of citizen observers across all regions, genders, and backgrounds, ensuring civic space monitoring reflects the full diversity of the continent.
