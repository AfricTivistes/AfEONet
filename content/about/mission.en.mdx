AfEONet’s mission is to promote democratic elections and strengthen electoral integrity across Africa through research, documentation, monitoring, and advocacy.

At the heart of our mission is the protection and advancement of citizen election observation as an essential component of democratic governance. We seek to establish a robust, evidence-based monitoring framework that tracks the operating environment for citizen observers, identifies emerging threats and restrictions, and documents instances where their rights, independence, or ability to observe elections are undermined. 

Citizen election observers are increasingly recognized as human rights defenders, playing an indispensable role in protecting civil and political rights, promoting transparency and accountability, and strengthening democratic values and principles.
