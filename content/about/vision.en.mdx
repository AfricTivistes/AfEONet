description: "The Africa AfEONet is working towards"

---

We envision an Africa where every election is observed by empowered, independent, and secure citizen observers — free to document, report, and defend the integrity of democratic processes without fear of reprisal.

A future where civic space is open in every country on the continent, and citizen observation is recognized, protected, and fully integrated into the democratic fabric of African nations.
