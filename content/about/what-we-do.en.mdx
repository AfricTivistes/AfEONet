### Election Observation & Learning Mission

We support and coordinate the deployment of citizen observers and experienced election observation teams to electoral processes across Africa. Through election observation, monitoring, and learning missions, we document electoral practices, assess the environment in which citizen observers operate, identify emerging challenges and good practices, and generate evidence to strengthen electoral integrity and citizen participation.

### Civic Space Monitoring
We monitor the conditions in which citizen election observers operate across Africa, using an evidence-based eight-dimension framework to assess civic space, identify restrictions and threats, and document developments that affect observers’ ability to operate independently, safely, and effectively throughout the electoral cycle.

### Research

We generate credible research, evidence, and analysis on the legal, regulatory, administrative, political, and civic environment for citizen election observation. We document trends, lessons, good practices, restrictions, and emerging threats to strengthen understanding of the conditions required for effective and independent citizen observation.

### Capacity Building

We strengthen the capacity of citizen observer organizations, focal persons, and observation teams to plan, deploy, monitor, document, analyze, and report on electoral processes and civic space. Through training, technical support, peer learning, and shared tools, we promote consistent, credible, and evidence-based observation across the continent.

### Advocacy

We translate evidence from observation, monitoring, research, and learning missions into strategic advocacy and policy engagement with electoral management bodies, governments, regional and continental institutions, civil society, and other stakeholders. We advocate for enabling legal and institutional frameworks that protect citizen observers, safeguard civic freedoms, and strengthen meaningful citizen participation in democratic processes.
