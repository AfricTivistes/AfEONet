### Election Observation & Monitoring

We track the state of civic space for citizen election observers across Africa, using an eight-dimension framework to assess the conditions in which they operate.

### Research

We produce reports and analysis on the regulatory, administrative, and political environment surrounding citizen election observation.

### Capacity Building

We train and support focal persons and observer organizations to collect, validate, and report on civic space data.

### Advocacy

We engage electoral management bodies, governments, and civil society to strengthen legal protections and open civic space for observers.
